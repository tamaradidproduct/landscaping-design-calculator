# Plot fill and surface quantities Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fill the whole plot with one surface that follows the plot boundary, and count every surface by its visible area in the Plant list.

**Architecture:** The fill is an ordinary surface outline flagged `base: true` whose shape the tool copies from the boundary on every update (`syncFill`, pure, in a new `src/plot-fill.ts`). It is kept last in the surface order so everything above cuts it, and the existing `visibleShapes` already clips to the lot and cuts by features and higher surfaces; `shapeArea` of that visible shape, converted to square feet, feeds new `areas` on the Plant list.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vite, vitest, polygon-clipping (already used), Playwright checks (`?store=memory`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-plot-fill-design.md`

All commands run in `plan-builder/`. Never push, open a PR or enable auto-merge (project CLAUDE.md).

## Global Constraints

- **Model:** optional `Outline.base?: true`, surface only, at most one per plan, otherwise an invalid plan; `v: 1` kept. The fill's id is `fill`, its name `Plot fill`.
- **Cutting:** the area of a surface is the visible part: clipped to the plot boundary, cut by existing features (Outlines) and any surface above it in Layers. Plants, objects, lines and markers never cut.
- **Order:** within Surfaces, earlier = higher. The fill is always the **last** key among surfaces.
- **Fill card:** name, material (Change), Texture scale, the note "Follows the plot boundary", Delete. No Width × Depth, rounding, nudge, Square corners, Redo, order arrows; no drag of corners, sides, bends or the body.
- **List rows:** "<Material> · 1,240 sq ft" (whole feet, thousands separated); zones of one material sum; no scale shows the material only; a wholly covered zone counts 0 sq ft.
- **Undo:** adding and deleting the fill are one step each; syncing is never a step of its own.
- **Commits** end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

- Redrawing the boundary while a fill exists: the fill keeps its last shape until the boundary closes, then re-fits; cancelling the redraw leaves both as they were.
- Undo and redo across a boundary edit, fill, delete and refill restore the fill in step with the boundary (never a stale shape).
- A stretched plan (calibration correction ≠ identity): a fill with nothing on top reports the same area as the boundary itself.
- A saved plan with `base` but no boundary, or two `base` outlines, is rejected or repaired without a crash.
- A zone drawn partly outside the lot, or covering the whole fill, gives sensible areas (clipped, or 0) and the fill stays last.
- Hidden zones still cut and still count; deleting the zone above a fill gives the area back.

### Task 1: Pure fill model, ordering and validation

**Files:**
- Create: `src/plot-fill.ts`, `src/plot-fill.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/outline.ts` (the `Outline` type, `moveInSection`, `sectionPosition`), `src/plan-model.ts` (validation), `src/outline.test.ts`, `src/plan-model.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `Outline.base?: true`.
  - `export const FILL_ID = 'fill'` and `fillOutline(boundary: Outline, material: MaterialId): Outline` (id `fill`, name `Plot fill`, kind `surface`, `base: true`, `closed: true`, points, bulges and radii copied (deep) from the boundary, `material` set).
  - `syncFill(outlines: Record<string, Outline>, boundaryDrawing: boolean): Record<string, Outline>`: returns a new map; with no closed boundary and no redraw in progress the fill is removed; with the boundary being redrawn (`boundaryDrawing` true) or unclosed, the fill is left as it is; otherwise the fill's `pts`, `bulges`, `radii` become copies of the boundary's, and the fill is moved to the end of the key order (`keepFillLast`). No fill: the map is returned unchanged.
  - `keepFillLast(outlines: Record<string, Outline>): Record<string, Outline>` (the fill's key last; others keep their order).
  - `moveInSection` returns null for the fill and when the neighbour it would swap with is the fill; `sectionPosition` ignores the fill (a zone just above it is `last: true`; the fill itself is `{ first: true, last: true }`).

  - `outline.test.ts`: `moveInSection` refuses to move the fill and refuses to swap a zone with it; `sectionPosition` for the fill is `{ first: true, last: true }` and for the zone above it `last: true`.
