# Plan Builder: plant and object symbols (2026-10-17)

## Decisions
- **Plants and objects together** from the Figma library: 51 plants and 14 objects (the PRD's "~47 / 17" was stale; the Objects section also holds three report variants that are not symbols).
- **One symbol per tap**; placing stays armed until Done or Esc. Plants and objects can **rotate** (15° steps or a typed angle).
- **Sizes researched online**, each with a source URL and the range found (`docs/symbol-sizes.md`); 15 are flagged approximate (an assumed cultivar or type, or a drawn length). A plant is a circle of its typical spread. An object keeps its researched long side and takes the other from the Figma art's proportions (the planter is about 4 × 1.7 ft).
- **Plan art** comes from Figma's 4× renders of each card's plan-view symbol (transparent background made by flood fill, shadow kept as translucent grey); photos are 184 px screenshots; the picker uses 64 px thumbnails so full art loads only when placed.
- **Plant list in the app** (symbol, name, category, quantity, photo; objects without category and photo; surfaces used). The water-consumption table is left for later.
- Placed symbols are **saved in the plan** (`symbols`) and share the eyes: a kind's eye and the section's eye, both saved. They are in the undo snapshot; visibility is not undoable.
- Canonical names: Figma's component, card and title names disagree, so each symbol has one id and name in `scripts/symbol-sources.mjs`, keyed by the card's node id.
