# Plan Builder: plot fill and surface quantities (2026-10-19)

## Decisions
- **Fill the plot** adds one surface that covers the whole plot and **follows the boundary** (not a one-time copy): reshape, bend or redraw the boundary and the fill re-fits.
- **One fill per plan**, always the lowest surface; a flag `base` on a surface outline, so no new saved collection and `v: 1` is kept.
- **Quantities:** every surface is counted by its **visible area**: clipped to the lot, with the house, existing features and any surface above it cut out. **Plants, objects, lines and markers do not cut** ("just the surfaces"). Shown per material in the Plant list's Surfaces rows, summed over zones.
- Out of scope: several fills, a fill that ignores the boundary, filling inside a feature, a default material per plan, per-zone areas in the zone card.

## Build rulings
- The sync runs in `render()` (not only after a change), so the fill follows a boundary corner live while it is dragged; it is never an undo step of its own.
- The fill's edges are not tappable for a typed length (the boundary's are); it shows no corner or bend handles and cannot be moved, pushed, rounded, squared, resized, redrawn or reordered (a new fill is always shown, even into a hidden Surfaces section).
- Area arithmetic is a pure `surface-area.ts` over the tool's cached visible shapes; the list reuses `formatArea`.

## Later
