# Plan Builder: Surfaces, stage 1 (2026-10-15)

Distilled summary of the design session behind this PR. Current requirements: `docs/PRD.md` §6.8. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-surfaces-design.md`.

## Scope
- First slice is **visual only**: zones with a fill and a name. Quantities (sq ft per material) and a default lot material come later; quantities were recommended for this slice but deferred.
- **Materials:** a built-in starter list with flat colours and simple patterns, kept as data. The Servin symbol textures (needs an asset export) and a contractor's own list were considered and left for later.

## Layers
- Hardscape and softscape share one **Surfaces** section; separate trade sections were proposed and declined.
- Surfaces sit **above Outlines** in the panel because they (and later Objects & plants) are used far more than outlines. Cutting logic is independent of panel order: existing features always cut zones; balcony-style exceptions later.
- The plot boundary clips zones; the section waits until the boundary is closed.

## Drawing
- **Material first:** Add surface… → pick → draw. Shape-first and a paint bucket (fill between existing lines) were considered; the bucket is a later shortcut.
- Zones look **solid**, and **see-through only while selected or drawn**. Making every zone see-through while drawing was offered and declined.

## Sizes
- Outlines trace what exists, so a typed length **sets the scale**; zones are designed, so a typed length **sets the zone's size** and never touches the scale.
- All three resize modes are wanted, in stages: **stretch along the edge** (stage 1, keeps rectangles square), **Width × Depth** for rectangles (stage 2), **move one corner** (stage 3).

## Architecture
- A surface is a new kind of outline (with a material), so the drawing tool, snapping, curves, Square, undo, cards and cutting carry over. A general layer model waits for objects and plants, which are placed symbols rather than drawn shapes.

## Collapsible sections (follow-up)
- Surfaces and Outlines collapse; the collapsed header shows the count. The plot plan moved into Outlines and collapses with it, and isn't counted.
- State is remembered per device; Outlines doesn't collapse by itself once the boundary is closed. Selecting or drawing a layer opens its section.
