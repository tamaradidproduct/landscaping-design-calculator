# Add tools and material grid — decisions

Date: 2026-10-24 · Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-add-tools-and-materials-design.md`

## Decided
- A tool row (Add, Surface, Edge, Plant) at the top of the Layers panel replaces the global + Add and its three list pickers.
- Every tool opens the same popover: search, one row of tag chips, a two-column thumbnail grid. Add opens on All; the others on their own category tag.
- A typed query searches every category, whatever tag is active. Recent keeps the last 8 picks on the device only.
- Tools are dimmed with a hint until the plot boundary is closed and the scale is set.
- Surface starts drawing as soon as it is tapped (shape first); picking a material first applies it to that zone (material first).
- A zone closed without a material is "Surface N", hatched, "Not counted yet" with an Assign material button; it counts nowhere (Sheet, Preview, quantities).
- Change material, Assign material and the plot fill all use the same grid; a tap applies at once (one undo step).
- Outlines stay outlines: a custom feature's card has Duplicate as surface (not the House, not the boundary); it copies the shape into a new unassigned zone and opens its grid.
- The plot boundary's action is "Fill the plot"; it is also the first cell of the Surface grid while the boundary is closed and the plot not yet filled.

## Built as
- `search-index.ts` (one list over materials, symbols and line items; tags; Recent) and `tool-row.ts` (row, popover, routing callback).
- `outline-ui.ts`: `startSurfaceDrawing`, `pickMaterial`, `duplicateAsSurface`; `surfaces-ui.ts`: the shared material grid in cards; `layers-ui.ts`: Fill the plot grid and the duplicate action.
- Browser checks: `scripts/check-tools.mjs`; the other Riesberg scripts use `addVia(page, tool, name)` in `scripts/lib/add.mjs`.

## Left over

- Thumbnail art for line items and plan-view art size in the grid.
